Abstract

Real-world radiology is comparative and longitudinal: disease progression is assessed by comparing current and prior MRI scans across multiple anatomical views and sequences. The Time-Aware Multi-View MRI Benchmark evaluates whether foundation models can reason over these longitudinal changes rather than interpret isolated images. It brings together 3,920 expert-verified question-answer pairs from 890 patients and more than 3,200 MRI timepoints across seven longitudinal cohorts, spanning glioblastoma, brain metastases, neurodegeneration, and vestibular schwannoma. Across 16 vision-language models, the benchmark reveals persistent limitations in clinically important change characterization, temporal reasoning, and quantitative assessment.

Overview

The benchmark evaluates foundation models on five complementary tasks designed around longitudinal MRI interpretation and disease progression reasoning.

Benchmark Coverage

Temporal Reasoning · Disease Progression · Structured Localization Guidance · Temporal Sequence Ordering · Change Localization Over Time

Comparison with existing MRI benchmarks. Time-Aware MRI provides full coverage across all five reasoning dimensions.

Five Evaluation Tasks

Temporal Reasoning

Interpret interval changes across sequential scans, planes, and contrasts.

Disease Progression

Characterize growth, regression, or stability over time.

Structured Localization Guidance

Identify changed anatomy, boundaries, features, and confounders.

Temporal Sequence Ordering

Arrange scans chronologically from visible progression patterns.

Change Localization Over Time

Identify the timepoint and spatial region showing maximal change.
